Introduction

Our project was selected for the NYU Prototyping Fund Challenge, which culminated in a showcase demonstration to over 100 students and entrepreneurial faculty members along with other project teams.

Outcome

Successfully developed a functional prototype and pitched our project to the NYU Tandon community.

Issue

When travelling, it's a hassle to constantly raise and lower your phone just to take pictures.

Our Solution

SnapSpecs eliminates that friction — built specifically for glasses to capture the most natural view matching what your eyes see.

A single button press takes a photo with haptic feedback and uploads it directly to a Google Drive folder.

The Prototype

Our Raspberry Pi interfaces with a tiny camera module and handles all scripts on boot, ensuring full automation and a smooth user experience. The script handles button feedback control, photo relocation, and cloud transmission to Google Drive.

Next Steps

Some improvements we identified: making the system viable without Wi-Fi — since connectivity isn't always available while travelling — and reducing the overall size and weight to improve portability and give users a lighter, more comfortable feel.
