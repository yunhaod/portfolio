Introduction

The Amazonia Hackathon aims to combat deforestation through hardware development. The hackathon took place over 2 days, with workshops and panels hosted by Seeed Studio, Cadence Design Systems, and Nordic Semiconductor.

The Challenge

How can we design an IoT device for Libélula to combat deforestation through hardware innovation?

Outcome

Successfully pitched our project plan to the judges and won second place out of a pool of over 100 participants from around the world.

Our Solution

To detect early stages of deforestation, we identified changes in animal movement patterns as a key indicator. Our project used GPS modules attached to animals and trees, alongside CO₂ sensors to detect spikes from chainsaw usage. Data from the sensors undergoes cleaning and organization to produce timely, accurate notifications.

Our Project Submission consisted of:

  • Google Doc detailing our research and project planning
  • Google Slides explaining our project and pitching to judges
  • Scala code for dataset creation and cleaning
