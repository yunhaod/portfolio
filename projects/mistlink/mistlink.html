Overview

This project aimed to create a humidifier compatible with the Google ecosystem through the Matter protocol. The humidifier is controllable through Google Assistant on the Google Nest Hub and the Google Home app.

Final Device Design

Outcome

The humidifier reacts to commands from the Google Nest Hub and Google Assistant.

It can also be controlled through the Google Home app.

Hardware

An ESP32 was used to interface with the Matter protocol as well as the water atomizer.

The physical model has space to store the electronics, as well as an insertable water tank at the back.

Takeaways

Along each step in this project, it was fascinating to use one of the newest IoT protocols in a personal design. This project taught me more about testing, validating, debugging, and troubleshooting complicated systems.
